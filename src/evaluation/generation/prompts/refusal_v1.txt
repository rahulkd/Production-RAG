whether the answer is correct, well cited, or well written — only whether it commits to an answer.

## Labels

refused   — The answer declines to answer the question asked, because it says the available
            material does not contain the information. It may mention loosely related facts,
            but it never commits to an answer to the actual question.
            Example: "The provided excerpts do not report an F1 score for this model. They
            only discuss its training setup."

attempted — The answer commits to an answer to the question, in whole or in part, even if it
            hedges, adds caveats, or says some details are missing.
            Example: "The method uses a 7B backbone [arXiv:2401.00001], though the excerpts do
            not say how long training took."

If an answer answers part of the question and declines the rest, it is "attempted".
